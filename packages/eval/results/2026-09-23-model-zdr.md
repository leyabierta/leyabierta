# RAG models and reranker under OpenRouter Zero Data Retention — 2026-09-23

**Question.** After moving the RAG stack to OpenRouter (#172), the account enforces
Zero Data Retention (ZDR). Which low-cost models and which reranker should
`POST /v1/ask` use, given that every model must have a ZDR endpoint?

**Short answer.**

1. The default reranker (`cohere/rerank-4-fast`) has no ZDR endpoint: every call
   returns 404 and the pipeline silently keeps the fused order. A listwise LLM
   rerank on `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (ZDR) raises Hit@1 from 43.9% to
   67.1% on 82 citizen questions (McNemar p = 0.0002), for +$0.0015 and +1.2 s
   per question. This is now the default (`RERANK_BACKEND=llm`).
2. For synthesis, `openai/gpt-6-luna` and `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` scored
   higher than `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` with a blind Claude judge
   (9.2 and 9.3 vs 8.4 out of 10, n = 22), but with 2.5× the latency, and
   DeepSeek costs 2.5× more per answer. The synthesis default is unchanged.
   See [Recommendation](#recommendation).

Scripts: `packages/api/research/ab/2026-09-23-zdr/` (`retrieve.ts`, `rerank.ts`,
`synth.ts`, `judge.ts`, `summarize.ts`).

## Method

- **Local production replica.** `runRetrievalCore` from `packages/api` runs
  against a local DB snapshot and the int8 Qwen vector index (486,145 vectors,
  9,737 norms). The query embedding comes from OpenRouter (`qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b`)
  and the analyzer is `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`.
- **Stage 1** caches the full fused pool (≈80 candidates) per question, without
  rerank. Every rerank variant and every synthesis model is then compared on
  exactly the same candidates.
- **Stage 2** applies each rerank variant to the cached pool and then runs
  `applyLegalHierarchyBoost`, as production does. Metrics are norm-level:
  Hit@k is the share of questions where a gold norm appears in the first k
  articles. Hit@15 means a gold norm is inside the 15 articles the synthesis
  model sees.
- **Stage 3** runs every synthesis model on the same 15 articles (the
  LLM-rerank context) with the production `buildEvidence` + `SYSTEM_PROMPT` in
  JSON-schema mode (`synthesizeAnswer`) and verifies citations with the
  production `verifyCitations`.
- **Stage 4** is a blind judge: the local Claude CLI (`claude -p --model sonnet`),
  a different model family from every candidate. It sees one call per question
  with the evidence and all answers under shuffled labels.

**Retrieval set (82 questions).** Three sources, all with norm-level gold:

- 45 questions from `packages/eval/datasets/heldout/human-50.json` (the ones that
  have gold);
- 25 from `citizen-queries.json` (v2) that are not in human-50;
- 12 autonomic-jurisdiction citizen-voice questions from `citizen-queries-v3.json`.

**Synthesis set (23 questions).** Labour (5), housing (4), tax, social security
(2), inheritance, domestic violence, consumer (2), data protection, police and
phone searches, autonomic law (2), false premises (2) and one out-of-scope
question. The out-of-scope question ("mejor abogado de Barcelona") is declined
by the analyzer before synthesis, so 22 questions reach the models.

## Retrieval: rerank options

Full set, n = 82. The pool ceiling (a gold norm anywhere in the fused ~80
candidates) is 95.1%.

| Rerank | Hit@1 | Hit@5 | Hit@15 | MRR@15 | Rerank p50 / p95 | Rerank $/q |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (fused order: what production does today, because Cohere 404s) | 43.9 | 73.2 | 81.7 | 0.573 | 0 | 0 |
| **LLM rerank, `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`** | **67.1** | **78.0** | **86.6** | **0.725** | 1.24 s / 1.51 s | 0.00145 |

Exact McNemar test, LLM rerank vs none:

- Hit@1: 22 questions improve and 3 get worse, **p = 0.0002**.
- Hit@5: 11 vs 7, p = 0.48.
- Hit@15: 5 vs 1, p = 0.22.

One of the 82 calls returned unparseable output and fell back to the fused order.

A 12-question subset was also run with the dedicated cross-encoder. The budget
did not allow the full set, because it costs 3× the LLM rerank.

| Rerank (n = 12) | Hit@1 | Hit@5 | Hit@15 | MRR@15 | p50 | $/q |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 58.3 | 83.3 | 83.3 | 0.708 | 0 | 0 |
| LLM rerank, `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` | 83.3 | 83.3 | 91.7 | 0.839 | 1.25 s | 0.00132 |
| `qwen/qwen3-reranker-8b` (cross-encoder, 600-char docs) | 50.0 | 75.0 | 91.7 | 0.629 | 1.03 s | 0.00427 |

A 3-question smoke test also tried an LLM rerank on `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`
with reasoning off. It cost $0.0037/q (routed to $0.14–0.30/M hosts) and was not
better, so it was dropped.

## Answer quality: synthesis models

Context is fixed: the same 15 LLM-reranked articles for every model. The judge
scores each criterion from 0 to 2, for a total out of 10.

- **Cit. precision (list)** is the share of the model's `citations` array that
  `verifyCitations` matches strictly to an evidence article.
- **Inline** applies the same check to the `[ID, Artículo N]` citations inside
  the answer text.

Latency is end-to-end for the non-streaming JSON call.

**All 22 questions** (Mistral: the 16 it answered):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` | 22 | 8.41 | 1.64 | 1.23 | 1.77 | 1.95 | 1.82 | 75.0% (60/80) | 77.5% (79/102) | 2.1 s | 3.3 s | 0.00094 |
| `openai/gpt-6-luna` (reasoning `minimal`) | 22 | 9.23 | 1.95 | 1.41 | 1.91 | 2.00 | 1.95 | 67.3% (37/55) | 93.8% (60/64) | 5.3 s | 9.3 s | 0.00091 |

|---|---|---|
| gpt-6-luna vs flash-lite | 12 / 5 / 5 | 0.14 |

| gpt-6-luna vs deepseek-v4.1-flash | 8 / 12 / 2 | 0.50 |

**Observations:**

- **flash-lite** is fast and cheap but the least faithful of the three viable
  models. Examples:
  - it answers "sí" to "puedo salir del trabajo para ir al médico" without the
    nuance the articles give;
  - it mislabels a norm once;
  - it cites a wrong ID once (`BOE-A-2012-8806` for `BOE-A-2021-8806`).
- **gpt-6-luna** has the best fidelity (1.95/2) and inline citation precision
  (94%). Its answers are shorter, so completeness is lower. It was released
  2026-09-22.
- **deepseek-v4.1-flash** gives the most complete answers (2.00/2) but:
  - its fidelity is no better than flash-lite's;
  - its output is about 1,100 tokens, twice flash-lite's;
  - p95 latency is 37 s;
  - OpenRouter spreads it over 20 hosts, some FP4-quantized, which makes the
    list of data processors long.
- **mistral-small-2603** returned 429 ("temporarily rate-limited upstream") on
  7 of 23 questions, even after 4 retries, and scored lowest. It is not usable.

## Recommendation

- **Reranker.** Use the LLM rerank on `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (implemented
  as the default).
  - It is the only measured option that works under ZDR and clearly improves
    retrieval.
  - It adds no new data processor: Google already serves the analyzer and
    synthesis.
  - `cohere-or` stays available only as an opt-in. On a ZDR account it is a
    guaranteed 404 per request.
- **Analyzer.** Keep `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`. It costs $0.00014/q with a p50
  under 0.4 s and was not the bottleneck here.
- **Synthesis.** Keep `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` as the default for now. The
  strongest candidate to replace it is `openai/gpt-6-luna`:
  - same cost;
  - higher fidelity and inline citation precision;
  - but 2.5× the end-to-end latency, not yet significant at n = 22, and one day
    old.
  - Before switching, measure it on the streaming path (time to first token) and
    on a larger set. `callOpenRouterStream` also needs to pass
    `reasoning: {effort: "minimal"}`, or the model uses its default reasoning
    effort.

## Other findings

- **Embedding requests had no timeout.** `fetchWithRetry` in `embeddings.ts` had
  no timeout on OpenRouter embedding requests, and one stalled request hung the
  eval run indefinitely. It now uses a 30 s timeout per attempt, retried like a
  5xx.
- **Worker-pool crashes with several workers.** In this macOS eval environment
  (Bun 1.3.14), the vector worker pool sometimes crashed at start-up with 4
  workers (SIGBUS/SIGTRAP, or a TinyCC `#endif` error in FFI setup) and was
  stable with `RAG_VECTOR_POOL_WORKERS=1`. The problem was not seen in
  production (Linux .so).

### EU in-region routing (reference only; not a requirement)

- In-region routing uses `https://eu.openrouter.ai/api/v1` and needs a Business
  or Enterprise plan (see OpenRouter's in-region routing guide). This account gets
  `403 Regional routing not enabled for this account`.
- `qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b` has no EU endpoint, and no rerank model is
  EU-eligible.
- `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, `gpt-6-luna` and `mistral-small-2603` have EU ZDR
  endpoints. On the global domain they can be pinned with `provider.only`
  (`google-vertex/eu`, `azure/eu`, `mistral/eu`), and this worked on this account.
- `google/gemini-embedding-2` (EU ZDR endpoint) reproduces the stored
  `gemini-embedding-2` corpus vectors exactly: cosine 1.0000 on 4 sampled
  articles embedded in the original `[<norm title>]\n<article title>\n\n<text>`
  format. Those 483,983 vectors cover the same 9,738 norms as the Qwen store.

## Judge prompt

The judge's system prompt is appended to the Claude CLI system prompt. It is
passed as `JUDGE_SYSTEM` in `judge.ts`:

```
Eres un evaluador experto en derecho español y en comunicación con la ciudadanía. Evalúas respuestas de un asistente que explica leyes españolas a ciudadanos sin formación jurídica. El asistente SOLO puede usar los artículos proporcionados como fuente.

Para cada respuesta, puntúa de 0 a 2 cada criterio:

1. fidelidad (fidelidad legal): 2 = todo lo que afirma está respaldado por los artículos proporcionados y las citas [ID, Artículo N] apoyan de verdad la afirmación a la que acompañan; 1 = algún detalle no respaldado o una cita que no encaja, sin error grave; 0 = inventa datos, cifras, plazos o normas, o contradice los artículos.
2. completitud: 2 = responde a la pregunta de fondo con los matices relevantes que SÍ están en los artículos; 1 = responde pero omite algo importante que estaba disponible; 0 = no responde a lo que se pregunta.
3. claridad (lenguaje llano): 2 = empieza por la respuesta directa y se entiende sin saber derecho; 1 = correcto pero con jerga sin explicar o rodeos; 0 = difícil de entender para un ciudadano.
4. ortografia: 2 = español correcto, con tildes, ñ y signos de apertura (¿ ¡); 1 = algún error menor; 0 = errores frecuentes o falta sistemática de tildes.
5. declinar: 2 = comportamiento adecuado (responde si los artículos lo permiten; si la premisa es falsa la corrige; declina solo si los artículos no responden o la pregunta no es jurídica); 1 = discutible; 0 = declina cuando podía responder, o responde inventando cuando debía declinar.

Sé estricto con la fidelidad: comprueba las cifras y plazos contra el texto de los artículos.

Devuelve SOLO un JSON con esta forma exacta, sin texto adicional:
{"scores": {"A": {"fidelidad": n, "completitud": n, "claridad": n, "ortografia": n, "declinar": n, "nota": "máx. 25 palabras"}, "B": {...}, ...}}
```

The user message contains the question, the evidence text built by
`buildEvidence` (identical to what the models saw) and the answers labelled
A–D. The label order is shuffled deterministically per question, and the judge
never sees model names.

## Limitations

- The gold standard is norm-level, not article-level. Hit@k says the right law
  was retrieved, not the right article.
- n = 22 for synthesis is small: only the rerank result (p = 0.0002) and
  DeepSeek > flash-lite (p = 0.02) are significant.
- A single judge model was used, with no human spot-check.
- The DB is a local snapshot from 2026-07, not the production DB.
- The cross-encoder was only measured on 12 questions.

## Reproduce

```bash
export ZDR_EVAL_OUT=/tmp/zdr DATA_DIR=/path/to/data RAG_VECTOR_POOL_WORKERS=1
D=packages/api/research/ab/2026-09-23-zdr
bun $D/retrieve.ts                                   # stage 1 (≈ $0.0002/q)
bun $D/rerank.ts --variants none,llm:google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite
bun $D/synth.ts --context llm_google_gemini-2.5-flash-lite \
  --models google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite,openai/gpt-6-luna,deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash
bun $D/judge.ts --context llm_google_gemini-2.5-flash-lite --judge sonnet
bun $D/summarize.ts
```

The spend for the whole study was $0.33, tracked in the `ledger.jsonl` that
`shared.ts` writes. `shared.ts` stops making calls once `ZDR_EVAL_BUDGET_USD`
(default 0.38) would be exceeded.
